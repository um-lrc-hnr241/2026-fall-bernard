How this site works

Builds are our essays. Each one revises the last, so reading a member’s builds in order shows an argument changing its mind. The maze of builds shows what has been posted, ring by ring.

Journals are our weekly reflections. Every member answered the same prompts, so the journals page lines up all five answers to each prompt side by side.

Search sits at the top of every page and looks through every build and journal entry at once.
